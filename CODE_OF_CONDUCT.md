# Code of conduct

MyAPES Account is run by APES CIC. We want Discussions, issues and pull requests to be friendly, safe and useful for everyone.

## Our standards
- Be kind, patient and respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Use plain language and help newcomers.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, spam or advertising.

## Privacy
- Never post personal data: names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, or client, pet, case, consultation or medical details.
- Maintainers will hide or delete posts containing personal data straight away.

## Security
- Never report security problems in public. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use a private advisory: https://github.com/APESCIC/MyAPES.Account/security/advisories/new

## Polls and announcements
- Announcements, Releases and deploys, and Polls are started by maintainers only. Other posts there will be closed or moved.

## Enforcement
Maintainers (apescic-director) may edit, hide, lock, move or delete content, and may block people who break this code. Report concerns privately via the contact details on https://github.com/APESCIC or the APES CIC website.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
